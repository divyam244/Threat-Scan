          Under the hood

          How the scan works

          Three kinds of checks, one shared scoring model, and a deliberate limit on what a score can claim.

          01

          Links are inspected piece by piece

          Protocol, host, subdomains, the real registered domain, lookalike brand names, shorteners, risky endings and encoded tricks — each one contributes a visible reason.

          02

          Messages are read for pressure tactics

          Credential requests, payment instructions, remote-access apps, fake deadlines and reward bait are matched by category. Any links inside are scanned as links, not just counted.

          03

          QR codes are decoded, never opened

          Images are checked for file-type mismatch, then decoded locally at several scales. The payload is shown to you and passed through the same link or message checks.

          0–19 Low risk signals 20–49 Suspicious 50–100 High risk

          These bands describe warning signals, not a guarantee. A clean-looking link can still be malicious, and a legitimate small business can look unusual.
